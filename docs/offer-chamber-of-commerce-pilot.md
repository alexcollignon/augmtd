## What augmtd is

augmtd is an AI layer that sits across the tools your organisation already runs on — email, calendar, meetings, documents — and builds a shared understanding of how your work actually moves.

Emails get processed and triaged as they arrive. Meetings are transcribed and turned into structured follow-ups. Documents you work with are indexed and searchable. Multi-step processes — onboarding a member, organising an event, preparing a policy position — can be run collaboratively with AI embedded at each step.

Over time, the platform builds context: who you work with, what's in progress, what's been decided, what's outstanding. That context feeds into everything — draft generation, meeting prep, process planning — so you're not starting from scratch each time.

The whole thing runs on a private AI layer. Your data — member communications, trade intelligence, internal discussions — is processed through models that run in a controlled environment, not shared public infrastructure.

## The pilot scope

Based on our conversation, two workflows are the right starting point:

**Weekly intelligence brief**
A structured digest delivered every Monday: business and trade news from Portugal and Germany, plus updates from the specific sources your team actually follows. Over time this cross-references with your internal context — past discussions, ongoing processes, your network — so it gets more relevant as the platform learns your focus areas.

**Pre-meeting research**
Before a meeting, pull a brief from inside the platform: company and person background, recent news, past interactions from your own email and meeting history. One place, ready before you walk in.

These are new capabilities being built into augmtd, with you as the first user. That means a lower price and direct input into what gets built and how.
